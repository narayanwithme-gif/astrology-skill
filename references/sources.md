# Source register and citation practice

Separate computation references from traditional doctrine and modern interpretive material. Use free legally accessible texts. Links do not prove every rule; inspect the relevant section before citing. Never invent page/edition details. This package contains original instructions, not reproduced textbook chapters.

## Computation

- Astrodienst AG, **Swiss Ephemeris programmer's manual**: https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swephprg.htm — calc_ut flags, sidereal modes, houses_ex, Moshier limitations and licensing. Primary technical reference; accessed 2026-11-03.
- Astrodienst, **Swiss Ephemeris information**: https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swephinfo_e.htm — engine description; accessed 2026-11-03.
- Python Software Foundation, **zoneinfo**: https://docs.python.org/3/library/zoneinfo.html — historical timezone data, fold handling; inspect when resolving DST.
- PySwissEph: https://pypi.org/project/pyswisseph/ — Python wrapper version/license. Free local dependencies, no API key; pinned versions in requirements.txt. Swiss Ephemeris/pyswisseph use AGPL terms for the free route; retain license notices on redistribution, and review obligations before a public service.

## Western tradition

- Claudius Ptolemy, **Tetrabiblos**, public-domain English translation by J. M. Ashmand (1822), Book I for signs/planets/aspects and relevant later books for topics. Accessible transcription: https://sacred-texts.com/astro/ptb/index.htm . Check translation and section before attributing any rule. This is one historical school, not a source for all modern psychological interpretations.
- William Lilly, **Christian Astrology** (1647), Books I/II for traditional horary tools and Book III for natal methods. Use a lawful public-domain edition and inspect actual locators; do not cite as authority for techniques it does not contain.
- Astrodienst Astrowiki, https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Main_Page — secondary modern terminology reference; attribute as modern/secondary rather than ancient doctrine.

## Jyotisha tradition

- **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra**, traditionally attributed to Parashara. Accessible Sanskrit/English section navigation: https://www.siva.sh/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra . Relevant topic titles: Divisions of a Rashi; Bhava judgement; Karm Bhava; Yoga Karakas; Graha Drishti; Dashas; Antar Dashas. Chapter numbering varies across recensions/editions. Cite page/chapter only after matching the inspected edition.
- Dasha section example, chapter 46 verses 11–15 in this site's numbering: https://www.siva.sh/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra/46/11-15 (checked accessible 2026-11-03). Verify the specific statement against the actual verses; this URL is not authority for every dasha implementation convention.
- Varahamihira, **Brihat Jataka**, and Kalyanavarman, **Saravali**, for additional tradition-specific rules. Locate lawful editions/translations and verify relevant text before citing. Do not conflate their rules with BPHS.

Traditional attribution is not proof of one uncontested author/date. A modern translation may remain copyrighted even if its Sanskrit source is public domain. No universal “authentic rule” claim without specifying school/text.

## Claim labels

- Calculated fact: values from chart.json with settings.
- Traditional doctrine: named text/school/rule with verified locator.
- Modern interpretation: named modern convention or clearly labelled synthesis.
- Inference: the assistant's tentative connection to the user's situation.
- Not verified/not calculated: missing source or unimplemented technique, without invented numerical strength or result.
