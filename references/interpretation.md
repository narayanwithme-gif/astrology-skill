# Interpretation procedures

## Evidence chain

Use: computed placement → tradition/rule/source → contextual interpretation → modifying/contrary factors → user-relevant question. Confidence labels describe data/rule completeness, not empirical predictive reliability. Name conflicting schools; do not blend them to create false agreement. Do not retrofit the user's known occupation into claimed chart proof.

## Western natal

1. Identify Ascendant, its traditional ruler (modern co-rulers separately), Sun/Moon, angles and planetary angularity. Evaluate signs, houses and actual aspects with stated orbs.
2. Use traditional domicile/exaltation/detriment/fall only for the seven traditional planets unless a modern scheme is explicitly named. Retrograde, combustion, sect, reception and accidental strength are separate evaluations; implement/verify them before assigning a score.
3. Read each house's topics, cusp sign, ruler sign/house/condition, occupants and relevant aspects. Empty houses still have rulers. For whole-sign houses, MC may lie outside the 10th; retain both concepts.
4. Synthesize with no fixed destiny or personality diagnosis. Outer-planet narratives are modern interpretation, not classical doctrine.

## Jyotisha natal

1. Establish Rashi Lagna, Lagnesha, Moon/nakshatra, Sun and graha house lordships. Distinguish natural benefic/malefic labels from functional lordship for this Lagna.
2. Review each bhava, its lord's sign/bhava, relevant occupants and stated drishti. Read from Lagna primarily; Moon-based views supplement rather than overwrite it.
3. Evaluate verified own-sign/exaltation/debilitation/mulatrikona, retrogression, combustion and varga context. Exact degrees of exaltation, combustion thresholds and cancellation rules need explicit references. Exaltation alone does not erase difficult lordship; retrogression is not automatically good or bad.
4. For yoga/dosha: state rule, show every required condition, record matched/not matched/not assessable, then discuss exceptions, strength and timing. Distinguish yoga-condition matching from results. Do not claim every combination is a yoga.
5. D1 anchors natal interpretation. D9 adds its tradition-specific context and dignity considerations; it is not a replacement natal chart. D10 concerns career-specific context; do not transfer every D1 rule mechanically. Explicitly label school choices and time sensitivity.
6. Use MD/AD and requested gochara window with natal context. A dasha is not a transit; the same lord does not create identical events for everyone.

## Topic checklists (evaluate separately in each tradition)

- **Career/work/business:** Western MC/10th ruler, 2nd resources, 6th work, Sun/Saturn and aspects; Jyotisha 10th/lord, 2nd/6th/11th, Lagna/lord, verified D10 and dashas. Distinguish employment, ownership, actual skill and market opportunity. No guaranteed job date or one prescribed vocation.
- **Income/resources:** Western 2nd/8th and rulers, relevant Venus/Jupiter/Saturn; Jyotisha 2nd/11th/lords, expenditure 12th and relevant combinations/periods. No investment instructions or guaranteed returns.
- **Relationships:** Western 7th/lord, Venus/Mars/Moon and aspects; Jyotisha 7th/lord, relevant karakas without gender assumptions, D9 and dashas. No fixed marriage/divorce date, partner character verdict, or abuse justification.
- **Home/family/property:** 4th/lord/Moon and relevant aspects in the respective systems. Do not infer actual parental history without asking.
- **Education/creativity/children:** 3rd/5th/9th and rulers, Mercury/Jupiter as appropriate; Jyotisha relevant bhava/lord, D1 and verified specialized varga if requested. No fertility diagnosis or offspring-sex prediction.
- **Wellbeing/workload:** 1st/6th/12th and relevant factors as symbolic routines/recovery themes only. Do not predict illness/death, recommend stopping treatment, or infer mental health conditions.
- **Travel/foreign connections/meaning:** 3rd/9th/12th and rulers, relevant periods. Distinguish themes from promised migration/visa results.
- **Friends/teams/community:** 11th/lord plus relevant Mercury/Venus/Jupiter, contrasted with 5th/7th when appropriate.

## Compatibility

Ask the actual relationship question and obtain consent/context for provided birth data. Compute each chart independently. Western: compare specific inter-chart aspects with stated orbs, angle sensitivity and house overlays; composites require their own calculation and are not supplied by the natal helper. Jyotisha: inspect both natal relationship frameworks; Ashtakuta/Guna Milan requires a specified regional scoring table and verified implementation. No fabricated 36-point score, deterministic compatibility percentage or verdict on whether people should marry. Communication, consent and observed behavior remain practical context.

## Advanced requests

- **Electional/muhurta:** obtain location, interval, event constraints, daytime/sunrise conventions and school. Need event-search/sunrise/panchanga calculations; snapshot output is insufficient. Show ranked candidate intervals with reasons and practical scheduling constraints.
- **Horary/Prashna:** record when/where the question was understood and chosen tradition. Use a dedicated rule set; do not use the user's natal data as a substitute.
- **Rectification:** record documentary evidence and candidate time interval, then compare hypotheses. Never assert an exact corrected time from a few remembered events.
- **Jaimini/KP/Ashtakavarga/Shadbala/more vargas/annual techniques:** source the exact method and acquire/implement a verifiable calculation before interpretation. Show “not calculated” when unavailable. No invented strengths, sublords, Sahams, Ashtakuta or comprehensive yoga database.
- **Remedies:** only if requested, frame as cultural/spiritual practices. Prefer free optional reflective practices; no compulsory gemstone purchases, fear-based payments or efficacy guarantees.
