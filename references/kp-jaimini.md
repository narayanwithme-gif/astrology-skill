# KP and Jaimini

Request `"vedic": ["kp", "jaimini"]` under `advanced`. The systems use separate outputs and chart settings.

## Krishnamurti Paddhati

The engine computes a dedicated chart using Krishnamurti ayanamsha and Placidus cusps. It splits each 13°20′ nakshatra into nine segments in Vimshottari proportions, beginning with the star lord, and repeats the procedure inside the selected segment for the sub-sub lord. The output includes this chain for planets and house cusps and gives the natal house occupied by each cusp's sub lord.

Natal ruling-planet factors include the sign and star lords of the Ascendant and Moon and the civil weekday lord at the birth site. The output covers the natal factors and cusp lookup. Full KP significator chains and horary judgment require additional methods. See K. S. Krishnamurti's *KP Readers* for the underlying convention.

## Jaimini

The seven-karaka scheme ranks Sun through Saturn by degrees advanced within their own signs. The report assigns AK, AmK, BK, MK, PK, GK, and DK in descending order. On an exact tie, the calculator uses the engine's planet order.

Rashi Drishti treats movable and fixed signs as mutually aspecting except for adjacent pairs. Dual signs aspect the other dual signs. These aspects follow the sign rules in the [Jaimini Sutras, 1.1](https://jyotisha.vedicreader.com/s/shl?s=3b5f9054f00&sec=%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0+%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%AE). Chara Dasha is marked unavailable.
