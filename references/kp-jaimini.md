# KP and Jaimini (planned)

## KP (Krishnamurti Paddhati)
Requires KP ayanamsha, star/sub/sub-sub-lord tables, ruling planets and Cuspal
Interlink. Distinct engine mode from Lahiri; never substitute. Until
implemented, mark `unavailable/not implemented`.

## Jaimini
Requires Karakas (7/8-karaka scheme), Chara Dasha, and Jaimini aspects
(special aspects for movable/fixed/dual signs). Until implemented, mark
`unavailable/not implemented`. Do not use Parashari drishti as a substitute.
