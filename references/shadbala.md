# Shadbala (planned)

Six-fold strength (sthana, dig, kala, chesta, naisa, drik) per BPHS, plus
uchcha-bala, combustion (asta) and other degree-based measures. Requires
exact longitudes, sunrise data and aspect tables. Until implemented, all
strength statements in readings are sign-level placement statements only.
Test vectors required before status changes from `not assessed` to `computed`.
